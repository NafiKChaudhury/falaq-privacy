Falaq Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

The short version
  • Falaq has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your prayer records, bookmarks, Khatms, memorization progress and Tasbih history stay on your phone.
  • Prayer and Qibla calculations happen on your phone. Nearby masjid search sends the selected coordinates to our server and Prayers Connect.
  • Online searches and content requests share the information needed to deliver those services. We do not sell your personal records.

Who we are

Falaq is a Quran and prayer app made by Nafi Chaudhury in Toronto, Canada. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the Falaq developer.

Information that stays on your phone

Falaq saves your prayer settings, prayer records and make up prayers, Quran bookmarks and reading position, Khatm plans, memorization progress, Tasbih counts and history, reminder choices, and display preferences on your device. Personal worship records are not uploaded to a Falaq account or cloud sync. Selected location coordinates may be sent for nearby masjid search as described below. Deleting the app removes its local copy; backups you exported remain wherever you saved them.

Location

If you allow it, Falaq uses foreground location to calculate prayer times, Qibla direction and your time zone. These calculations happen on your phone. When you use nearby masjid search, the selected latitude, longitude and search radius are sent to Falaq's server, which forwards nearby search coordinates to Prayers Connect. Automatic location may supply precise coordinates; selecting a city supplies that city's coordinates instead. Optional travel suggestions check location while the app is active if permission was already granted. Falaq does not request background location tracking.

When you search for a city while online, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo's free place search to find matching cities. Falaq also includes a built in city list that works offline without sending anything.

Quran text, translations and recitations

Quran text, translations, Tajweed and recitation audio use Quran Foundation resources. When you open content that is not already saved on your phone, the app asks Falaq's server for it, and our server fetches the requested resource. Requests include the surah, verse, translation or reciter needed to deliver that content. Our server receives your connection's IP address. Quran Foundation receives the server connection for proxied requests; audio and other content hosts receive your connection directly where the app requests their files. Hosting and provider logs may retain request information under their own privacy policies. We do not promise that third-party requests leave no logs or that all providers use the same retention period.

Content you download for offline use is stored on your phone.

Hosted services and private test access

Falaq's Quran and masjid backend is hosted on Netlify at falaq-services-nafi.netlify.app. Netlify processes connection information and requests needed to deliver and protect this service, including IP addresses and requested routes. Requests can include selected nearby coordinates, search text, masjid IDs and Quran resource references. Hosting and provider logs may retain this information. We have not established a single retention period across these operators; see Netlify's Privacy Statement. The backend does not upload your personal worship records or run app analytics.

Online services connect automatically, without a personal account or shared access code. On first online use, Falaq's server issues a random identifier and secret credential for that installation. The app uses the credential to obtain signed service sessions that last one hour; it silently renews them while the installation credential remains valid, for up to one year. Neither value is an advertising identifier or hardware fingerprint. This identifier and your IP address are used only to deliver and protect app features, including request and abuse limits, not for advertising or analytics.

The iPhone keeps the credentials in its device-only Keychain. They are excluded from Falaq's personal backups and are sent only to the configured Falaq backend, never to Quran Foundation, masjid providers or audio/content hosts. Browser previews keep their credentials in memory. A non-secret local installation marker prevents reuse of an old Keychain identity after a normal uninstall/reinstall; iOS backup/restore and Keychain behavior can vary. Reset online connection in Settings removes the local credential and marker; a later online request obtains a new installation identity. Offline content and personal worship records remain available.

The backend validates signed installation sessions and uses keyed digests of installation identifiers and IP addresses for short-lived, in-memory rate counters. Counting windows last one minute; expired counter entries are removed on a subsequent request or when the server instance is recycled. There is no account database, persisted service-session history or app analytics history. These counters and credentials are not written to the public masjid cache. Netlify separately processes network information and may retain platform/security logs under its own policies; we do not promise that resetting the local identifier deletes those logs.

Masjid services

Masjid directory queries, selected coordinates and requested masjid schedules pass through Falaq's server to Masjidal, Prayers Connect or the legacy My-Masjid service as applicable. The server caches public listings, schedules and nearby results. Nearby cache entries are derived from search coordinates. Prayers Connect uses a server-side guest session; Falaq does not send your personal worship records with these requests. Provider links, map links and public images may contact their respective hosts. These operators may retain requests or technical logs under their own privacy policies.

The hosted backend caches public masjid directory and published schedule data in site-scoped Netlify Blobs, with directory freshness of about a day and schedules of a few hours, plus memory and temporary-storage fallback. Personal locations, access credentials, session tokens and rate counters are not written to this public store. Nearby search results derived from selected coordinates can be cached in memory or temporary storage. Freshness periods control refreshes rather than guaranteeing immediate deletion; expired public records are also subject to scheduled maintenance. Temporary files can remain while a hosting instance is reused. We do not promise a fixed deletion time for platform storage or logs.

Notifications

Prayer and other reminders are scheduled on your phone. No push service or notification token is used, and nothing about your reminders is sent to us.

Backups

If you make a backup, Falaq creates a file on your phone and you choose where it goes, for example Files, iCloud Drive or a message to yourself. We never receive your backup. Anyone you share the file with can read what's in it, so keep it somewhere private.

Links to other websites

Some guidance in the app links to outside sites such as sunnah.com. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

This privacy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no Falaq analytics, forms or advertising scripts. GitHub receives connection information when you visit, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. This website does not host the app's Quran or masjid backend.

Children

Falaq has no accounts, advertising or tracking features directed at children. Online requests can still send location, search text and connection information as described above. A city can be selected manually and bundled content can be used offline. Parents and guardians can manage location and notification permissions in the phone's Settings.

Your choices

You can turn off location access, notifications or both at any time in your phone's Settings. Deleting the app removes its local content and personal records; exported backups remain where you saved them. Use Reset online connection in Settings to remove the saved service credential and local installation marker. The next online request creates a new installation identity. This does not delete hosting/provider logs or public masjid data.

Changes to this policy

If Falaq starts handling information differently, for example by adding crash reporting or a feedback form, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
